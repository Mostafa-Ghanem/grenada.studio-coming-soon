## Project facts

**Shorfat Al Haram (Makkah)**: 914 residential & commercial plots; area over 1.3 million m² (confirmed by the team; the profile text omits the unit). Deliverables: landing page, social, outdoor, booklet, teaser + marketing videos, brochure, reels. Assets: 8064×4536 drone/site photos, social posts in 4320px.

**Malqa Taif**: 149 commercial–residential plots, Al Wasit district, Taif. Deliverables incl. influencer videos and a marketing plan.

**Khairat Taibah (Madinah)**: from its final report:
- Hybrid auction, licence 4586, FAL auction licence 120002344.
- 17 Sep 2025, Mubasher Auctions platform + Le Méridien Madinah hall. Organised by Aqar One under Infath supervision.
- 8 commercial & agricultural properties, 6,000 to 276,000 m².
- **25 participants, 293 visitors, 8 bids.**
- ~33 social posts, **~2 million views** from targeted audiences, **3,207 visits** to the auction page.
- Channels: X, Snapchat, TikTok, WhatsApp, YouTube, Google; TV spots (Fashion News, Ya Hala), Sabq and Nabd online papers, radio, road billboards, influencers (Nasser Al-Harthi, Ahmed Al-Farhan, and others).
- Report notes: bidders found the prices above market value, and there was weak demand for the agricultural lots, even though the marketing reached the target segments. One lot sold (6,395 m² residential-commercial, SAR 14,916,875). **Keep the sale details off the site**, because they name the buyer.

**Dream Land (Taif)**: hybrid auction, 322 residential & commercial plots. Assets: 3D renders (1408 px wide, so higher-res originals would help), drone video, final videos 1080p, digital-screen loops, invitation, chocolate stickers.

**Nukhbat Al Taif**: hybrid auction, 7 commercial/agricultural/residential lands in Taif (e.g. 1,075 m² residential land in Al Hada). Organised by Aqar One with Al-Nasiri & Al-Kraida law firm. Assets: installed on-site sign photos, drone per property, interest-registration sheet.

**Osoul Makkah**: final report PDF (410 MB) and source files are there, but not yet extracted. *Next step: pull stats from it like Khairat Taibah.*

**Basateen Al Madinah** (2 agricultural lands) and **Afaq Al Riyadh** (189,000 m² agricultural land and more, Jeddah & Khulais): candidates for new case studies.
