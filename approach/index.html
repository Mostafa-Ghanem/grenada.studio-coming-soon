How we work

Strategy to execution, without the handoff gap.

Grenada connects current digital marketing thinking with fully integrated field solutions, so the project story can stay coherent from positioning through measurement.

G
Operating model

4 stages. One connected team.

The sequence is simple on purpose. Complexity belongs inside the execution, not inside the client experience.

01
Position

Define the market story.

Audience, offer, competitive context, message hierarchy and the narrative the project needs to own.

02
Build

Create the campaign system.

Identity, content, landing experiences, media assets, outdoor and sales-support material.

03
Launch

Activate every channel.

Paid media, social publishing, creators, on-ground execution, event coverage and lead capture.

04
Optimize

Measure and improve.

CRO, KPIs, ROI, analytics, reporting and continuous refinement across the campaign lifecycle.

The principle

Every discipline should know what the project is trying to move.

That shared commercial context is what lets design, content, media, web and production behave like one campaign instead of parallel deliverables.

Start at stage one

Bring us in before the handoffs begin.

The earlier the positioning, creative and execution teams share context, the stronger the campaign system can become.
